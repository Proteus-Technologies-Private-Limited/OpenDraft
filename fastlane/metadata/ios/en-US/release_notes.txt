HEAR YOUR SCRIPT READ ALOUD
Table Read reads your screenplay out loud, with a different voice for each character and a narrator for the action. Follow along as each word is highlighted on the page, pause, skip scenes, or change the speed.

A VOICE FOR EVERY CHARACTER
Each character gets their own voice automatically, or pick one yourself on their profile. Your choices are saved with the script.

FREE VOICES OR AI VOICES
Use the voices already on your device, free and offline, or try AI voices: Kokoro, free and private on your device, or Google Gemini, OpenAI, ElevenLabs and Microsoft Azure with your own account. AI voices other than Kokoro send your script's text to that company, so the app reminds you whenever one is in use.

ALSO IMPROVED
You can now choose separately whether scripts and opened files save automatically.
Leaving a script can no longer save an empty page over your work.
