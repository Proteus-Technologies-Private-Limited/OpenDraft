Keep writing your cloud scripts without a connection, and share a project on this device with your other devices.

CLOUD SCRIPTS OFFLINE
A cloud screenplay you have opened once now opens without a connection. Your changes are saved on the device and upload when you are back online. If someone changed the script elsewhere in the meantime, nothing is overwritten: you choose which version to keep.

SYNC A PROJECT FROM THIS DEVICE
Turn on Sync with OpenDraft Cloud for a project on this device, and its screenplays reach your other devices with the same account. If a screenplay was changed in both places, you pick the one to keep and the other is saved as a copy, so nothing is lost.

FOLDABLES HELD LIKE A LAPTOP
Half open with the crease across the screen, your toolbar sits in two rows beside the crease and keeps your choices.

ALSO IMPROVED
Asking before you delete something works again, arranging your workspace by keyboard is safer, an iPad shows as an iPad in your list of devices, and switching accounts is smoother.
