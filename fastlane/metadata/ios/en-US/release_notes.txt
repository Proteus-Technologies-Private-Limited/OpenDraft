Everything new since 2.2.0, for iPad and iPhone.

AV SCRIPTS FOR COMMERCIALS, CORPORATE AND DOCUMENTARY
Write in two columns — picture on one side, sound on the other — with shot numbers and timings that look after themselves. Give a shot a duration and every start time below it moves to match. The running total is your runtime.

STORYBOARD INSIDE THE SCRIPT
Any row can hold a frame: a picture of the shot, at 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 9:16 or 2.39:1. Your boards sit beside the words they belong to, and they travel with the script.

COLUMNS THAT FIT YOUR SCRIPT
Make the video or audio column as wide as it needs to be. What you set is what you get on the page, in the PDF and in the spreadsheet.

INTERVIEWS AND NARRATION, WRITTEN PROPERLY
Character, dialogue, parentheticals, lyrics, action and shots can all go inside the columns now — so an on-camera interview or a piece to camera reads like a screenplay instead of a note in a box.

HAND YOUR SCRIPT TO A SPREADSHEET
Send an AV script out to Excel or CSV for a shot list, and bring a producer's spreadsheet back in — OpenDraft works out which column is picture, which is sound and which is timing. PDF and Word come out as a proper table, with the headings repeated on every page.

WRITE AV SCRIPTS WITH YOUR FINGERS
No hardware keyboard needed. Add and remove rows from the toolbar or a long press, and step out of the table onto an ordinary line whenever you want to write something that is not a shot.

ALSO FIXED
The PDF now breaks its pages exactly where the editor does, so your script is paginated the way you wrote it. A picture added to an AV row becomes that row's storyboard frame instead of splitting the table.
