Tidy up your drafts, and PDFs that read right in Indian languages.

DELETE A DRAFT FROM THE FILE MENU
File > Delete Script removes the script you have open, whether it's on this device or in OpenDraft Cloud. Type its name to confirm, and you're taken back to its project.

YOUR PROJECTS, EASIER TO FIND
Manage Projects is now in the File menu, and the Open screen links to it, so renaming and deleting projects and drafts is always close at hand. When you delete a project's last draft, OpenDraft offers to remove the empty project too.

TAMIL AND OTHER INDIAN LANGUAGES IN PDF
Tamil now looks the same in a PDF as it does on screen. Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam and Sinhala get the same fix, with joined letters printed properly.
