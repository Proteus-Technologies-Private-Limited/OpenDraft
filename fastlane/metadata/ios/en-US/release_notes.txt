Build your characters and places in detail, see everything about the scene you are writing, and open OpenDraft again on older iPhones and iPads.

CHARACTERS, LOCATIONS AND SCENE DETAILS
Give each character a profile with pictures, their story, voice, relationships and the costumes they wear. Give each location a description, looks such as Day and Night, and reference pictures. Scene Details shows the scene you are in: where it is set, who is there and what each of them wears. Your existing profiles and pictures carry over.

WORKS ON iOS 15 AGAIN
If OpenDraft stopped at "Loading timed out" on iOS 15 or an early version of iOS 16, it opens normally again.

PLANNING VIEWS ON FOLDABLES
On a foldable held open, the page view, index cards, outline and beat board open beside the script.

EASIER BY KEYBOARD
Customising your workspace now works fully from the keyboard.

ALSO IMPROVED
Typing in long scripts is faster, panels fit better on smaller screens, and you can rate OpenDraft from the Help menu.
