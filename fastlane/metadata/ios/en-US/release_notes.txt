Made for iPhone Duo, plus Script Health and spellcheck in several languages.

MADE FOR IPHONE DUO
OpenDraft now arranges itself for each way you hold your iPhone Duo. Folded, your script fills the cover screen. Open it like a book and the script takes one page, with your panels on the other. Prop it half open like a laptop and your panels sit below the script. The layouts you pick folded and open are remembered separately.

SCRIPT HEALTH
A quick check finds empty scene headings, unfinished dialogue and notes you haven't dealt with. It runs on your device and needs no account.

SPELLCHECK IN SEVERAL LANGUAGES
Writing in more than one language? Tick every dictionary you need and they all check at once.

EASIER WITH A KEYBOARD OR SCREEN READER
Menus, panels and dialogs can all be worked from the keyboard, and notifications are read aloud.

ALSO IMPROVED
Long scripts stay quick while you type, and printing without the title page leaves it out again.
