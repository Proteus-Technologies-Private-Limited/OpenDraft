New tools for writing, rewriting and sending your script out.

BRING YOUR SCRIPTS WITH YOU
Import a script you only have as a PDF, or a Highland, WriterDuet or Celtx file. Before a PDF opens, you can check how each line was read and fix several at once.

WATERMARKS, PASSWORDS AND SIDES
Put CONFIDENTIAL, or each reader's name, on every page, and add a password. Print or export just the scenes an actor needs on their original pages, with the rest crossed out, two pages to a sheet if you like.

REPORTS
Scene, character, location, cast, breakdown and notes reports, as a spreadsheet or a PDF.

REWRITE WITH EASE
Rename a character everywhere at once. Read and polish everything one character says in the Dialogue Tuner. Keep other versions of a line or a whole scene beside it and switch between them, from Edit > Alternates.

PLAN YOUR STORY
Name your plotlines, put scenes in them, and see them all across the script on the Story Timeline. Link Beat Board beats to the scenes that carry them.

COMIC BOOK SCRIPTS
A new format for comics and graphic novels, with pages and panels numbered for you.

NOTES YOU CAN DISCUSS
Reply to notes, resolve them, assign them to a collaborator and @mention people.

KEEP WRITING
Daily goals, timed sprints and a calendar of what you wrote. Focus Mode with typewriter scrolling. Bookmarks, and macros for text you type often.
