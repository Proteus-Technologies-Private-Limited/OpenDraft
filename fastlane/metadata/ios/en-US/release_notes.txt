Write in your own language, and keep your colours when you export.

OPENDRAFT IN 18 LANGUAGES
Menus, dialogs and settings now speak Spanish, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Turkish, Arabic, Persian, Hindi, Urdu, Marathi, Gujarati, Telugu, Tamil, Odia, Punjabi, Malayalam, Kannada and Bengali. OpenDraft follows your device's language, or pick yours in Settings > Language. Arabic, Persian and Urdu read from right to left, and your scripts never change when you switch.

COLOURS AND HIGHLIGHTS IN EXPORTS
Coloured and highlighted text now stays that way in Word and PDF. Want a plain black-and-white copy? Switch colours off when you export a PDF.

DELETED MEANS DELETED
When you delete a saved version of a script, its text is now erased from your device too.

SMARTER SCENE HEADINGS
SmartType now understands intros like DREAM SEQUENCE: before INT. or EXT., suggests longer prefixes you've saved, and completes your own scene times.

ALSO FIXED
The AI Assistant and Dictation add-ons are back in Settings > Add-ons.

TELL US WHAT TO IMPROVE
The translations were made with AI and are still being reviewed by native speakers. If a word could be better, we want to hear it.
