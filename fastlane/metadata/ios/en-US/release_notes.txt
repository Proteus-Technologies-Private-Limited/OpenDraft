CHARACTER VOICES ARE KEPT
The voice you choose for a character in Table Read now stays with them when you close and reopen your script. If you picked voices in the last update, please choose them once more.

FROM THE LAST UPDATE: HEAR YOUR SCRIPT READ ALOUD
Table Read reads your screenplay out loud, with a different voice for each character and a narrator for the action, highlighting each word as it's spoken. Use the free voices on your device, or AI voices: Kokoro, free and private on your device, or Google Gemini, OpenAI, ElevenLabs and Microsoft Azure with your own account. AI voices other than Kokoro send your script's text to that company, and the app reminds you whenever one is in use.
