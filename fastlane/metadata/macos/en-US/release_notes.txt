See your whole story at a glance, print just the scenes you need, and write by voice.

THE OUTLINE
See your acts, sequences and scenes in one list, with each scene's length. Drag a scene to move it, and group scenes into sequences.

PRINT ONLY THE SCENES YOU NEED
Export a range of scenes, or pick any you like, and choose the title page, page numbers, scene numbers and a watermark for that copy.

DICTATION
Speak your scene, check the words, then add them to your script. Simple voice commands start a new scene or a character's line. It's off until you turn it on, and can work entirely on your Mac.

AI ASSISTANT
Turn it on to have an AI model running on your own Mac write a line or a scene for you. Nothing goes into your script until you approve it.

FASTER TYPING
Character names, locations and transitions you've already used are offered as you type.

SEE WHAT CHANGED BETWEEN DRAFTS
Comparing drafts now shows rewritten lines word by word, and which lines moved to another scene.

YOUR OWN SCRIPT FORMATS, EVERYWHERE
Custom elements are quicker to make, and a script keeps its custom format when you open it on another device or share it with a co-writer.

ALSO NEW
Suggest bold, italic and other styling for a co-writer to accept. Line numbers can be locked for a recording or shooting script. Long speeches continue neatly onto the next page.
