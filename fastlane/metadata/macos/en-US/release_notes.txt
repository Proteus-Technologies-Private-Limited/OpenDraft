PANELS IN THEIR OWN WINDOWS
Pop out any panel, such as the Navigator, Notes, Character Profiles or Scene Details, into its own window and put it on another display. It is the same live panel, several can share one window as tabs, and each opens where you last left it.

CLOUD SCRIPTS OFFLINE
A cloud screenplay you have opened once now opens without a connection. Your changes are saved on your Mac and upload when you are back online. If someone changed the script elsewhere in the meantime, nothing is overwritten: you choose which version to keep.

SYNC A PROJECT FROM THIS MAC
Turn on Sync with OpenDraft Cloud for a project on this Mac, and its screenplays reach your other devices with the same account. Nothing is lost if a screenplay was changed in both places.

WINDOWS OPEN WHERE YOU LEFT THEM
OpenDraft remembers the window's size, display and full-screen state.

ALSO IMPROVED
Arranging your workspace by keyboard is safer, a repeated error is shown only once, and switching accounts is smoother.
