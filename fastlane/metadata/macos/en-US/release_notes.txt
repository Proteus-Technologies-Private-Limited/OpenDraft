RUNNING TIME TO THE SECOND
The running time estimate now shows minutes and seconds, so even a short film or a single scene gets an exact figure.

KNOW HOW LONG YOUR FILM WILL RUN
The running time estimate is now far more accurate. It reads your pages the way a production does, about one page to a minute, and lands close to the real running time of released films.

TELL OPENDRAFT HOW YOUR FILM PLAYS
Choose a pacing for your script: Brisk for fast talk and quick cuts, Measured for slow-burn and lingering moments, or Standard. Each one shows its running time before you pick. Click the estimate to change it any time.

TAG YOUR SCRIPT'S GENRES
Pick as many genres as fit, or add your own. They're saved with your script.
