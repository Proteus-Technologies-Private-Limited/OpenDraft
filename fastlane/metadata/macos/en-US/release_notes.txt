YOUR WORKSPACE, YOUR WAY
Put the Navigator and every panel in the left, right or bottom dock, choose what sits on the toolbar and status bar and in what order, and save layouts you can switch between in one click. Whatever doesn't fit in the window is one click away, just above the status bar.

ONE ZOOM CONTROL
Zoom with buttons, a slider or both, and fit the page to the width of the window in one click. Keep it on the status bar or move it to the toolbar.

SPLIT SCREEN
Keep the printed page, your index cards, the outline or the beat board open beside the script.

SPEED VIEW
Write in one continuous scroll without page breaks. Your page count and exports stay exactly the same.

STORY MAP
See your whole story as a strip of scenes, sized by pages or screen time, and jump to any scene.

A HANDIER NAVIGATOR

LINE SPACING AND FONT SIZE
