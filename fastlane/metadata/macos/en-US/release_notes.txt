Everything new since 2.1.0.

YOUR OWN TEMPLATE AS THE DEFAULT
A template you made could be applied to the script in front of you and nowhere else: every new script still started in one of the built-in formats. Your templates are now offered alongside those formats, and Make default — in the template manager, or in Format > Script Format Preferences — puts one at the top of the New Screenplay picker, already selected, so Return takes it. The picker still asks, because keeping several formats means the next script could be any of them.

DUPLICATING A FORMAT KEEPS THE FORMAT
Duplicating a built-in format copied its fonts and margins but left behind the starter pages, act breaks, title-page fields and dialogue spacing that make it that format. A copy now carries all of it.

THE UPDATE NOTICE SAID YOU WERE UP TO DATE
Running 2.0.0 with 2.1.0 already on sale here, the app told you there was nothing new. The Mac and iOS App Stores share one product page but approve and publish separately, so either can be a release ahead — and the check that watches them asked once and took the iPhone and iPad answer for both. Each store is now asked for itself, and a store that cannot answer leaves the notice looking rather than being told the wrong version.
