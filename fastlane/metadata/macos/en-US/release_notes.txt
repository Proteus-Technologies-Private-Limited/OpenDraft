WRITE IN YOUR OWN SCRIPT
A screenplay written in Hindi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Sinhala, Tamil, Telugu or Thai now exports to PDF with a typeface that can draw it. These used to come out as blank space where the dialogue should have been. Chinese, Japanese and Korean arrive the first time you need them.

HEBREW AND ARABIC, THE RIGHT WAY ROUND
Both now export right to left, with Arabic letters joined the way they are written rather than standing apart.

WORD FILES THAT KEEP YOUR SCRIPT
A Word export now asks Word for a typeface that can draw what you actually wrote instead of a Latin one, so the Hindi in your script is still Hindi when a producer opens it.

WHEN A TYPEFACE WILL NOT LOAD
The export now tells you which one, instead of quietly leaving a gap where your words should be.
