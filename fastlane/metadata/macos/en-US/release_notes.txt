Write in your own language. OpenDraft's menus, dialogs and settings now speak 18 languages.

NEW LANGUAGES
Spanish, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Turkish, Arabic, Persian, Hindi, Urdu, Marathi, Gujarati, Telugu, Tamil, Odia, Punjabi, Malayalam, Kannada and Bengali.

PICK YOURS IN SECONDS
OpenDraft follows your device's language. To change it, open Settings > Language and tap your language, shown in its own name.

RIGHT TO LEFT
Arabic, Persian and Urdu flip the whole app to read from right to left. Your script keeps the format's layout, and nothing in your scripts or exports changes when you switch language. Your Mac's menu bar is translated too.
