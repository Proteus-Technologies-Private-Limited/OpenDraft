Exporting works again, and highlights behave in Word.

EXPORTING AND SAVING FIXED
Exporting your script, Save As and saving to a file you opened work again. If OpenDraft ever replaces a file another app made, the old copy is kept for you under File > Recover Original File.

HIGHLIGHTS YOU CAN REMOVE IN WORD
Highlights in a Word export are now real Word highlights, so you can change or clear them in Word like any other. Prefer your exact colours? Turn that on in Settings.

SMOOTHER TYPING
Long scripts stay quick while you type and delete.

ALSO FIXED
Scene numbers of two or more digits no longer stack up in the margin, and signing in for the first time now points you to Create Account.
