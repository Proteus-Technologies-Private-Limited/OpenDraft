REVISIONS THE WAY A PRODUCTION WORKS
Turn on Revision Mode and every line you change gets a mark in the margin, in the PDF and in print. Choose your own mark, or a different one for each revision, so the Blue and Pink changes can be told apart.

COLOURED REVISED PAGES
Print revised pages tinted in their revision's colour, or export just the revised pages to send to cast and crew.

LOCKED PAGES AND SCENE NUMBERS
Lock your pages and new text goes onto A pages instead of moving every page after it. With scene numbers locked, a new scene after scene 12 becomes 12A, and a cut scene can stay in the script marked OMITTED.

FINAL DRAFT REVISIONS COME WITH YOU
Revisions now travel both ways with Final Draft, and are marked in Word, Fountain and Fade In files too.

DARK PAGES
A new theme that turns the page itself dark for writing at night. What you print and export stays black on white.

ALSO FIXED
Importing an exported project no longer brings every script in twice.
Treatments always open as treatments.
