New tools for writing, rewriting and sending your script out.

IMPORT A PDF
Bring in a script you only have as a PDF. Scene headings, characters, dialogue, dual dialogue, scene numbers and the title page all come back as screenplay elements.

WATERMARKS, PASSWORDS AND SIDES
Put CONFIDENTIAL, or each reader's name, on every page, and add a password. Export just the scenes an actor needs on their original pages, with the rest crossed out.

REPORTS
Scene, character, location, cast, breakdown and notes reports, as a spreadsheet or a PDF.

REWRITE WITH EASE
Rename a character everywhere at once. Read and polish everything one character says in the Dialogue Tuner. Keep other versions of a line beside it and switch between them.

PLAN YOUR STORY
Name your plotlines, put scenes in them, and see them all across the script on the Story Timeline.

NOTES YOU CAN DISCUSS
Reply to notes, resolve them, assign them to a collaborator and @mention people.

KEEP WRITING
Daily goals, timed sprints and a calendar of what you wrote. Focus Mode with typewriter scrolling. Bookmarks, and macros for text you type often.
