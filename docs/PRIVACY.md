# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: April 8, 2026**

## Overview

WriTea Draft is a screenwriting application developed by Proteus Technologies Private Limited, available on macOS, iOS, iPadOS, Windows, Linux, and Android. Your privacy is important to us.

## Data Collection

**WriTea Draft does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.** The application runs entirely on your device.

Specifically:

- **No analytics or tracking** — We do not use any analytics services, crash reporters, or tracking tools.
- **No user accounts required** — No registration, login, or authentication is required for core functionality.
- **No cloud storage** — All your screenplays and project data are stored locally on your device.
- **No advertising** — WriTea Draft does not display ads or use advertising identifiers.

## Network Connections

WriTea Draft works fully offline. The only optional network feature is **real-time collaboration**, which requires connecting to a collaboration server. When using collaboration:

- You choose which server to connect to (self-hosted or the public demo server).
- Only your screenplay content and a display name are transmitted to the collaboration server during an active session.
- No data is retained on the collaboration server after the session ends.
- Collaboration is entirely optional — all other features work without any network connection.

## Data Storage

Your screenplays, projects, and settings are stored locally on your device:

- **macOS:** `~/Library/Application Support/net.japanada.writea-draft/`
- **iOS/iPadOS:** App sandbox (managed by iOS, accessible via Files app)
- **Windows:** `%APPDATA%\net.japanada.writea-draft\`
- **Linux:** `~/.local/share/net.japanada.writea-draft/`
- **Android:** App-private storage

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it. You can delete it at any time by removing the application and its data folder.

## Third-Party Services

WriTea Draft does not integrate with or send data to any third-party services. No SDKs from third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking providers are included in the application.

## Children's Privacy

WriTea Draft does not collect any data from any users, including children under 13. The app is safe for use by all ages.

## Your Rights

Since WriTea Draft does not collect personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete. Your screenplay files are entirely under your control on your device.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the changes here with a revised date. Since WriTea Draft does not collect data, we do not anticipate significant changes.
